Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Stashday is a personal savings planner. This page explains what happens to the information you put into it.

Without signing in

Your pay, spending list, savings balance and goal are stored only in your device’s browser storage. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in turns on sync, so the same plan appears on every device where you sign in. When you sign in:

  • Google confirms who you are. Stashday receives your Google account’s ID, name and email address.
  • Your Stashday data (the same numbers and notes you enter in the app) is stored in a Google Cloud Firestore database, run through Firebase, in Singapore.
  • Security rules let only your signed-in account read or change your data.

What we don’t do

Stashday has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don’t sell or share your information, and we don’t use it for anything except showing it back to you.

Network requests

The app downloads its own files, a typeface from Google Fonts and, for sync, the Firebase libraries from Google. Like any website, those servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete my cloud copy removes your synced data from the database. Settings → Erase everything deletes it on your device too, and in the cloud if you’re signed in. Without sync, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything.

Children

Stashday is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
