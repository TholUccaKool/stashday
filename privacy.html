Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Stashday is a personal savings planner. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: it never leaves your device.

What the app stores

The numbers you enter, such as your pay, your monthly spending list, your savings balance and your goal, are saved only in your device’s browser storage, inside the app. Stashday has no account system, no server database and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. Stashday does not collect, send, sell or share any personal or financial information. We cannot see your data, and nobody else can through us.

Network requests

When it first opens, the app downloads its own files and a typeface from Google Fonts. Like any website, those servers receive standard technical information, such as your IP address. They do not receive anything you type into Stashday.

Backups

If you use Back up, the app creates a file on your device. Where that file goes afterwards, for example if you send it to yourself, is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase everything in the app, or uninstall the app or clear its storage. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes it completely.

Children

Stashday is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
